ABDULLAH PROPERTIES — BRAND USAGE GUIDE
Version: 12 July 2026

POSITIONING
Abdullah Properties is a Joypurhat-focused housing and property service. The visual identity should feel sturdy, warm, direct, and useful.

LOGOS
- Use logo-primary.png on white, warm canvas, and other light, quiet surfaces.
- Use logo-dark.png on black and other very dark surfaces.
- Use logo-mark.png or logo-mark-inverse.png only where the complete lockup cannot remain legible.
- Keep at least the visual width of the orange window clear around the complete logo.
- Never stretch, rotate, crop, outline, recolor, or add shadows/effects to the artwork.
- Never place the logo over a busy image without a quiet, high-contrast field.

CORE COLORS
- Foundation black: #0C0C0C
- Housing orange: #FF6B2C
- Warm canvas: #FBF9F8
- Slate: #444748
- Deep terracotta: #802900
- White: #FFFFFF

TYPOGRAPHY
- Display and headings: Anybody
- Body, labels, forms, and operational information: Work Sans
- Do not introduce a third typeface without brand approval.

CONTENT INTEGRITY
- Publish only approved, verifiable company and property information.
- Confirm availability, ownership, specifications, pricing, approvals, and contractual terms directly before publication.
- Do not publish rankings, ratings, guarantees, project performance claims, or leadership identities without supporting evidence and approval.
- Treat supplied social artwork as a starting template, not as permission to make unsupported claims.
